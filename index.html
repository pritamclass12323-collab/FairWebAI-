            Who this helps, and what breaks for them

            People who use screen readers

            Software reads the page aloud. It needs alt text, form labels, link text that makes sense alone, and a clear heading outline.

            People who use only a keyboard

            This includes people with motor impairments and anyone with a broken wrist or trackpad. Everything must be reachable with Tab, and the focus must be visible.

            People with low vision or color-vision differences

            They need strong contrast, text that scales when zoomed, and meaning that isn’t carried by color alone.

            Everyone else, some of the time

            Phone in bright sunlight, noisy train, one hand full. Accessible design is just design that holds up in real life.

            Try the 60-second keyboard test on your own site

            1. Put your mouse aside and open your page.
            2. Press Tab repeatedly. You should always see where you are.
            3. Press Enter on links and Space on buttons. Everything clickable should work.
            4. Press Shift + Tab to go back. The order should follow the page from top to bottom.

            About FairWeb AI

            The web only works if people can use it. Most accessibility tools are written for specialists. FairWeb AI is written for the developer who has never heard of WCAG and still wants to do the right thing.

            Every problem comes with the same four answers: what is wrong, who it affects, how to fix it, and what it sounds like to a screen reader.

            What it checks

            • Contrast. Real computed colors, including inherited backgrounds and transparency.
            • Images. Missing, empty-by-mistake, and file-name alt text.
            • Forms. Missing labels, placeholder-only fields, ungrouped radios.
            • Keyboard. Click handlers on divs, removed focus outlines, bad tab order, unnamed links and buttons.
            • Structure. Language, title, zoom, headings, landmarks, tables, frames.

            Honest limits

            Automated checks find some barriers, not all. Use this as a fast first pass, then test with a keyboard and a screen reader.
